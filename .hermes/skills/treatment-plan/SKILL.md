## Purpose
Generate branded treatment plan PDFs for MMV Medical patients.
Never send without human approval.

## Required information before generating
- Patient full name
- Treatment type and scope
- Implant brand tier (Macros / Nobel / Straumann)
- Number of units
- Bone graft required (yes/no)
- Sinus lift required (yes/no)
- Estimated timeline (days in Istanbul)
- Price range (from Dr. Mehmet only)

## PDF structure
1. Patient name and date
2. Recommended treatment summary
3. Step by step procedure breakdown
4. Timeline and stay duration
5. What is included (transfers, hotel suggestions, aftercare)
6. Price range — never a fixed final price
7. Next step — book a free video consultation

## Branding
- Navy and gold color scheme
- Dr. Mat International Dental Clinic header
- Dr. Mehmet Akif Türkan credentials footer
- DejaVu fonts — avoid Turkish dotted-I issues

## Never do
- Send the PDF without Jared's approval
- Include a fixed final price
- Include competitor comparisons in the document
- Generate a plan without complete patient information

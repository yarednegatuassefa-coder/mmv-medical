# Clinical and treatment-marketing claims approval register

## Purpose and release rule

This register is the evidence and approval gate for public treatment-page claims, pricing, savings comparisons, clinical descriptions, provider credentials, treatment timelines, and facility/licensing statements.

**No claim is approved merely because it is present on the website.** A claim may be published only after the supporting evidence is current, traceable, and approved by the accountable clinical owner and the appropriate compliance/legal reviewer. Marketing may edit for clarity but cannot create or broaden clinical meaning.

This document does not provide clinical advice and does not itself approve any current website statement.

## Approval workflow

1. **Inventory:** copy the exact public wording into the register. Record page, component, language, and date.
2. **Substantiate:** attach the source document or authoritative reference, its issuer, effective date, scope, and expiry/review date.
3. **Clinical review:** the clinic's named clinical director or appropriately qualified clinician verifies clinical accuracy, limitations, and patient-specific caveats.
4. **Commercial review:** the commercial owner verifies current prices, included/excluded items, currency, tax, and comparison methodology.
5. **Compliance/legal review:** review advertising, medical-tourism, licensing, consumer-protection, and cross-border claims for the intended audience and jurisdictions.
6. **Publish:** only the approved exact wording is released. Keep evidence and approval metadata with the change.
7. **Revalidate:** review at least every 90 days and immediately after a price, provider, supplier, protocol, facility, or regulatory change. Expired evidence returns the claim to pending.

## Pricing comparison method

For every savings statement:

- Compare the same procedure and unit count, not a single implant against a full restoration.
- Compare equivalent materials, prosthesis, imaging, consultations, temporary/final work, and aftercare.
- State the comparator geography and the date the comparator was collected.
- Keep the original currency and disclose the exchange-rate date and method if converted.
- Calculate savings as (comparator price minus MMV price) divided by comparator price, multiplied by 100.
- Use a defensible range where prices vary; do not present the maximum observed saving as typical.
- Do not imply that travel, accommodation, revision care, complications, or local follow-up are included unless documented.

## Required evidence record

For each claim, retain:

- Exact approved wording and translations
- Public page/component and target market
- Source document or official URL, issuer, document date, effective date, and expiry/review date
- Procedure, material, provider, facility, or price scope covered by the evidence
- Known exclusions, caveats, contraindications, and patient-specific dependencies
- Clinical approver name/title and approval date
- Commercial approver name/title and approval date (where applicable)
- Compliance/legal reviewer and approval date
- Publication commit/deployment reference
- Next review date and change owner

Do not place patient-identifiable information, medical records, or private staff credentials in this register.

## Immediate review of the current treatment page

The current public page contains multiple claims across all the categories above: price points, UK comparators, savings percentages, inclusions, brand names, provider qualifications, facility licensing, treatment durations, superiority language, popularity, and outcome-style descriptions.

**Treat all of these claims as unverified pending documentary review.** This is not a finding that each claim is false; it means the repository does not currently contain the evidence needed to substantiate them.

Until approvals are recorded, do not add new numerical savings, clinical guarantees, credential claims, or guaranteed timelines. The page owner and clinic should decide whether to temporarily remove, qualify, or retain each statement after the review—not an engineer acting without the evidence.

# MMV Medical Marketing Playbook
Use with AGENTS.md, CLAUDE.md, failures.md, privacy/consent rules, and clinical/operations review. Strategy only; no campaign is live.

## Audience / job
International prospective patients and family decision-makers seeking clear information about medical-travel coordination in Turkey. Prioritize English, Arabic, or French only where the complete inquiry/follow-up journey can reliably support that language. They need process, requirements, boundaries, and a low-pressure human response.

## Positioning hypothesis
A clear multilingual starting point for understanding and coordinating medical-travel inquiries in Turkey, with human follow-up and transparent next steps. Validate with non-identifiable research. Do not claim superior clinical quality, savings, outcomes, success rates, or unverified credentials.

## Message pillars
Clear coordination steps; human guidance; transparency between coordination and clinical advice; language support only where genuinely available.

## Funnel / assets
Educational explainer → process/checklist/FAQ → CTA to request human coordination response → approved, consent-based follow-up. First screen states audience, what coordination means, and CTA. Include privacy notice, consent, contact route, and clinical/non-clinical boundary. No fear-based targeting, thin doorway pages, diagnosis, or unreviewed prices.

## Measurement / approval
Aggregate visit → CTA → valid inquiry → qualified inquiry → human-reviewed booking progression. Never send names, contacts, diagnoses, treatment details, passport data, or message contents to analytics/models. Clinical review for medical statements; privacy review for forms/tracking; owner approval before publication. Patient messages and CRM writes remain gated.
